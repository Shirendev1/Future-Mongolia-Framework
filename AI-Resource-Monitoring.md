# AI Resource Monitoring System

Use artificial intelligence and digital monitoring to make Mongolia's natural resource extraction and exports transparent.

## Goals
- Analyze geological and satellite data to identify mineral deposits
- Monitor mining production in real time
- Track exported minerals and resource flows: what leaves the country, where it goes, and who gets paid
- Improve transparency and help prevent illegal extraction
- Monitor environmental impact to protect ecosystems
