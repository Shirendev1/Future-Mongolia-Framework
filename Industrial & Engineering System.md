# Industrial & Engineering System

## Vision

Develop Mongolia into a regional center for heavy equipment engineering, maintenance and remanufacturing, supporting the mining sector and advancing circular industrial practices.

This system aims to shift Mongolia's industrial capacity from import dependency toward engineering knowledge, technological capability and sustainable industrial development.

## Background

Mongolia relies heavily on imported heavy equipment and spare parts for its mining and infrastructure sectors. Maintenance, repair and component replacement often require long supply chains and high costs.

A domestic engineering and remanufacturing ecosystem would reduce operating costs, create skilled jobs and build technological capability within the country.

## System Components

### 1. Heavy Equipment Engineering Center

Advanced diagnostics, repair and optimization services for heavy machinery used in mining and construction.

Key capabilities:
- Hydraulic system repair and optimization
- Gearbox rebuilding
- Electronic diagnostics and control systems
- Predictive maintenance technologies

### 2. Remanufacturing Facility

Recover and restore used mechanical components, extending equipment life cycles and reducing industrial waste.

Typical components:
- Hydraulic pumps
- Cylinders
- Bearings
- Structural mechanical components

This process follows the principles of the circular economy and industrial sustainability.

### 3. Engineering Training and Innovation Center

Train Mongolian engineers and technicians in modern industrial technologies.

Focus areas:
- Hydraulic engineering
- Industrial automation
- Smart maintenance systems
- Industrial diagnostics and monitoring

The system could seek cooperation with international engineering and equipment companies, for example Bosch Rexroth, Caterpillar, Komatsu or Hitachi Construction Machinery. These are examples of potential partners only; no partnerships exist.

## Expected Outcomes

- Reduced dependence on imported spare parts
- A national engineering ecosystem
- Skilled technical jobs
- Growth of circular industrial practices
