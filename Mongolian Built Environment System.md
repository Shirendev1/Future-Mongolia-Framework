# Mongolian Built Environment System

## Vision

Develop a human-centered and climate-adaptive construction standard for Mongolia.

## Key Principles

1. **Human-scale cities** — prioritize walkability and community spaces rather than high-rise density.
2. **Climate-adaptive architecture** — buildings must respond to Mongolia's extreme climate.
3. **Circular construction** — reuse materials and use low-carbon construction methods.
4. **Regional design** — architecture should reflect Mongolia's different ecological regions.
5. **Smart infrastructure** — integrate energy monitoring, water recycling and digital infrastructure.

## System Components

1. **Urban planning model** — human-scale city design with mixed-use neighborhoods.
2. **Construction standards** — energy efficiency, insulation and structural resilience adapted to Mongolia's climate.
3. **Green infrastructure** — green corridors, urban forests and water systems.
4. **Regional architecture** — design guidelines for each ecological region.
5. **Digital infrastructure** — smart monitoring of energy, water and environmental impact.

## Ger District Transformation

Transform Mongolia's ger districts step by step into modern eco-districts, without forced relocation, while keeping community identity and improving living conditions.

1. **Infrastructure first** — clean water, sewage, district heating, electricity and road access.
2. **Gradual housing upgrade** — help residents move from traditional housing to insulated eco-houses at their own pace.
3. **Green neighborhood design** — green streets, tree corridors, parks and pedestrian-friendly public spaces.
4. **Renewable energy** — solar panels and energy-efficient heating.
5. **Community-centered development** — local residents take part in planning.

## Sustainable Housing Communities

Develop low-density residential neighborhoods that fit Mongolia's lifestyle and climate.

1. **House-based communities** — eco-friendly houses instead of high-rise apartment blocks.
2. **Human-scale neighborhoods** — walkable streets with parks, playgrounds and shared spaces.
3. **Energy-efficient homes** — strong insulation, renewable energy and cold-climate building techniques.
4. **Green living environment** — trees, gardens and small parks within neighborhoods.
5. **Local services** — schools, childcare, shops and healthcare within walking distance.
