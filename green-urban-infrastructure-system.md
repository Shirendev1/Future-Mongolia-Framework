# Green Urban Infrastructure: Lessons from Europe

Mongolian cities, especially Ulaanbaatar, face air pollution, traffic congestion, limited green space and pressure on water and waste systems. European cities, including those in the Czech Republic, offer practical examples Mongolia can learn from.

## Key Areas

### 1. Wastewater Treatment
Modern biological treatment plants clean wastewater before it returns to rivers, protecting water quality and public health.

### 2. Urban Green Infrastructure
Parks, green corridors and urban forests are planned as part of the city. They cool streets, clean the air and give people places to rest.

### 3. Sustainable Waste Management
Households separate waste at source, and collection and recycling systems turn much of it into new materials instead of landfill.

### 4. Public Transport
Trams, metro and electric buses make it easy to live without a car, which reduces traffic and air pollution.
